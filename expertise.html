Our Fibre

Renewable biomass fibre

The Wilson System® processes biodegradable materials into a consistent fibre that can be evaluated for industrial applications.

Potential applications

Renewable fuels

Feedstock for processes such as fermentation to produce acetone, butanol and ethanol.

Sustainable aviation fuel

A potential biomass input for emerging aviation fuel pathways.

Paper and packaging

A recovered fibre input for paper, cardboard and moulded packaging.

Renewable heat and power

A potential fuel for combined heat and power systems.

Biochar and construction

A feedstock for producing biochar that can be evaluated for use in concrete and other materials.
