Recovering value from biodegradable waste

Gateway Green Recycling is an Alberta-based company developing a new source of renewable fibre for Canadian manufacturers.

Commercial demonstration plant

Edmonton Waste Management Centre

The Edmonton Waste Management Centre will host Gateway Green’s first Canadian commercial demonstration plant. The project will test the Wilson System® autoclave in an operating environment and evaluate recovered fibre with research and industry partners.

How the demonstration works

  1. Source biodegradable materials

    Use feedstocks such as food and green waste.

  2. Recover fibre

    Process the material in the Wilson System® autoclave.

  3. Test applications

    Evaluate the recovered fibre for use in fuels, materials and other bioproducts.
